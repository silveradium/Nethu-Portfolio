01ABOUT

About Me

I'm a compère and public speaker — and a fashion designer at heart.

The stage is where I feel most at home: hosting events, holding a room and keeping the energy moving from the first welcome to the final bow. I love speaking about ideas I care about and connecting with every audience I face.

Off the mic, I design wearable pieces made by hand that carry colour, culture and a story. I also model, and give my time to volunteer work that puts people first.
